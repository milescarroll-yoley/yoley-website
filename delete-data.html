Delete your data

Yoley: Invoice & Quote App  ·  Yoley Limited  ·  Last updated 13 September 2026

In short: Yoley has no user accounts and no Yoley server. Everything you create in the app is stored on your own device, so you can delete all of it yourself in seconds from inside the app, or by uninstalling. The only data held anywhere else is a merchant account application, if you chose to make one.

1. Delete everything in the app

This removes every record the app holds on your device.

  1. Open the Yoley app.
  2. Open the menu and tap Settings.
  3. Scroll to the bottom and tap Clear all data.
  4. Confirm when asked. The app restarts empty.

Uninstalling the app also removes its database and settings from your device.

Android backup

On Android, the app takes part in Android's standard backup, which copies its database to your own Google Drive. That copy belongs to your Google account, not to Yoley, and clearing data in the app does not remove it. To delete it, go to your device's Settings › Google › Backup, or manage it at drive.google.com under app data. You can also turn the backup off there.

iPhone iCloud sync

On iPhone, records sync through your own iCloud account. Deleting the app's data removes it from your devices; you can also manage or remove the app's iCloud storage in Settings › your name › iCloud › Manage Storage.

2. Ask us to delete a merchant account application

If you applied for a card-processing account from inside the app, that application was sent to Monek Merchant Services Limited, our payment provider. It is not stored by Yoley, and clearing the app's data does not withdraw it.

To have it deleted, email privacy@yoley.com from the address you applied with, with the subject Delete my data. Tell us your business name and, if you have it, the reference shown when you applied. We will pass the request to Monek and confirm back to you. We respond within 30 days.

3. What is deleted, and what is kept

DataWhat happens
Customers, quotes, invoices, expenses, purchase orders, job diary, subcontractors, settings and brandingDeleted immediately when you use Clear all data, or when you uninstall. Held only on your device.
Receipt photos and scanned textDeleted with the app's data. Never uploaded — scanning happens on your device.
Your device backup (Google Drive or iCloud)Kept until you delete it, because it lives in your own cloud account. See the steps above.
Merchant account application (name, date of birth, address, business and bank details)Deleted on request — email us as above. Monek is an FCA-authorised payment institution and is legally required to keep anti-money-laundering records for five years after a business relationship ends, so some details may be retained by Monek for that period even after deletion.
Emails you have sent usDeleted on request, unless we need them to handle a dispute or meet a legal obligation.

Yoley holds no advertising profile, no analytics and no tracking data about you — there is none to delete.
