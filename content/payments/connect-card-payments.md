## Steps

1. Wait for the approval email from Monek — it contains your **Merchant ID** and **API Key**.
2. In Yoley, go to **Settings → Yoley Money → Connect**.
3. Paste your **Merchant ID** and **API Key** into the fields shown.
4. Tap **Connect**.

Yoley probes the Monek gateway with your credentials before saving them. If they're valid (or if Monek's servers can't be reached at that moment), the credentials are saved and you can start sending payment links straight away. If Monek explicitly rejects the credentials, you'll see:

> *Monek didn't recognise these details. Check your Merchant ID and API Key against the email from Monek (or the Monek Portal) and try again.*

The fields stay on screen so you can correct and retry without re-opening the form.

> **Tip:** Copy and paste rather than typing — a single transposed character is enough for credentials to be rejected.
