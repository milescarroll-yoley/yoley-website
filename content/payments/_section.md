Yoley card payments are powered by **Monek Merchant Services Limited**, an FCA-authorised UK payment institution. Apply in minutes from **Settings → Yoley Money** — your business details are pre-filled from your Yoley profile.

## What's included

- **Payment links** — send secure card payment links by text or email.
- **Apple Pay** — your customers can pay with Apple Pay.
- **Tap to Pay on iPhone** — coming soon.

> **Note:** Card payments are provided under agreement with Monek Merchant Services Limited, an FCA-authorised payment institution. Your data is sent directly to Monek and is not stored by Yoley.
