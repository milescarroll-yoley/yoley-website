## Overview

Yoley processes card payments through **Monek**, a UK payment services provider. You apply once from inside the app; Monek reviews and approves within one working day.

## Steps

1. Go to **Settings → Yoley Money**.
2. Tap **Set up your Yoley Card account**.
3. Work through the four steps — the form pre-fills from your Yoley profile where possible. Check every field and correct anything that doesn't match your official business details.
4. If you'd rather start with a blank form, tap **Start blank instead** on the pre-fill banner.
5. On the **Settlement Account** step, enter the sort code and account number where card-payment funds should land.
6. On the final **Review & Submit** screen, check all your details, tick the consent box, then tap **Submit Application**.
7. After approval, Monek emails your **Merchant ID** and **API Key** to the address you entered. Paste both into **Settings → Yoley Money → Connect**.

> **Tip:** Your application is checked against the Monek gateway when you tap **Connect** — if the details are wrong you'll see an error straight away with the details still on screen, so you can correct them without starting over.

## Saving and resuming your application

You don't have to complete the application in one go:

- Tap **Save & finish later** on the review screen (shown when any required field is still missing) to save your progress to this iPhone's secure Keychain.
- Yoley will send you a local reminder the next day.
- To continue, go to **Settings → Yoley Money** — the row changes to **Finish your card account application** when a saved draft exists. Tap it to pick up exactly where you left off.
- Tapping **Cancel** / **Back** from the first step also saves your draft automatically if you've entered anything.

## Required information

Have the following ready before you start:

- Business name, address, trade / industry and (if VAT-registered) VAT number
- Trading name if it differs from your registered business name
- Limited company number (limited companies only)
- Your full name, email address, phone number and date of birth (for identity verification)
- Home address, if different from your business address
- UK bank sort code (6 digits) and account number (8 digits) for the settlement account

> **Note:** Your data is sent directly to Monek and is not stored by Yoley. Monek is a UK payment services provider regulated for the purpose of merchant onboarding.
