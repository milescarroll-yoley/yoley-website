## Rates

| Card type | Rate |
|---|---|
| Consumer (personal) Visa / Mastercard | **0.99%** |
| Business Visa / Mastercard | **1.99%** |
| Amex | May carry a small surcharge |

The rate is applied by Monek at settlement. Because the app cannot know the card type at the time you send a payment link, any fee **estimate** shown in the app uses the consumer rate (0.99%).

## When you are charged

- You pay only on **successful** transactions — money that actually reaches your bank.
- Declined cards cost nothing.
- There is **no monthly fee** and **no application fee**.

## Apple Pay and Google Pay

Customers can pay with Apple Pay or Google Pay — these are treated as consumer card payments.

> **Tip:** Custom rates are available for high-volume merchants. Contact Monek directly to discuss.
