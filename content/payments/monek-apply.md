## What you need before you start

- Your business name and trading address
- Your VAT number and/or Companies House number (if applicable)
- Director or owner full legal name and date of birth
- Home address of the director/owner
- UK bank sort code and account number for payouts

## Steps

1. Go to **Settings → Payments** and tap **Apply for Monek**.
2. **Business** — confirm your business name, trading name and address.
   - Tap **Find address** → enter your house name or number, then your postcode → tap **Find →** to auto-fill the address fields.
3. **Personal** — confirm your name, email and phone number.
4. **Director / Owner details** — enter the full legal name of the director or owner.
   - For limited companies, Yoley looks up the name on the Companies House register and pre-fills it — you can edit it if needed (the register sometimes shows only an initial).
   - Enter the director's date of birth. Limited-company applicants see a menu picker pre-filled with the birth month and year from Companies House — just select the day.
   - If the director's home address differs from the business address, toggle **Different address** and use the address finder.
5. **Bank** — enter your UK sort code and account number. Payouts settle the next working day.
6. **Review** — check all details including the director name, then tick the consent box:

> **Note:** By submitting you consent to your application details and device-session information being sent to Monek Merchant Services Limited. Monek will check your details with a credit reference agency and verify the identity of applicants for anti-money-laundering and fraud-prevention purposes.

7. Tap **Submit Application**.

## After you apply

- You will receive an email from **monek.com** confirming receipt and keeping you updated.
- Most applicants are approved within **24 hours**.
- Once approved, paste your credentials into **Settings → Payments → Connect** to start sending payment links.
- Need help? Call Monek UK support on **03445 269 6645**.
