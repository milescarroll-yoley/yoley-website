## Steps

1. Tap the **Settings** tab → **Social & Messaging** → **WhatsApp & Facebook Messenger**.
2. Under **WhatsApp**, enter your work number (e.g. `07…` or `+44…`).
3. Tap **Test — open WhatsApp chat** to verify the link opens correctly.
4. Under **Facebook Page**, enter your Page name (e.g. `YoleyPlumbing`) — omit the `@` and any `facebook.com/` prefix.
5. Tap **Open my Messenger inbox** to jump straight to Meta Business Suite, or **Test — open my Page chat** to verify your `m.me` link.
6. Your details are saved automatically as you type.

> **Tip:** Once your WhatsApp number is saved, every customer with a phone number shows a **Chat on WhatsApp** button in their contact details, and enquiry leads in the inbox get a quick-tap WhatsApp icon.

> **Note:** This feature uses direct deep links (wa.me / m.me) — no backend or Meta login is required. Full inbox ingestion (Facebook and Instagram enquiries appearing directly in Yoley) is coming soon; adding your details now puts you first in line.
