## Overview

The **Trading Report** gives you a month-by-month summary of your sales, purchases, VAT position, and CIS subcontractor costs — all from data already in Yoley.

## Opening the report

1. Tap the **menu** icon (top left) to open the side menu.
2. Tap **Trading Report**.
3. Use the **‹** and **›** arrows to move between months.

## What the report shows

**Trading summary**
- Sales (ex VAT), Sales VAT, Purchases (ex VAT), Subcontractor costs (gross)
- **Net profit** = Sales (ex VAT) − Purchases − Subcontractor costs

**VAT summary**
- Sales VAT (output), Purchase VAT (input, reclaimable)
- **VAT due to / reclaimable from HMRC** for the period

**CIS summary**
- Gross paid, CIS deducted, and net paid per subcontractor
- Only shown if subcontractor costs are logged for the month

> **Note:** Sales and purchases are dated by when they were raised or incurred in Yoley; subcontractor costs are dated by when they were logged. Figures cover only what's recorded in the app.

## Exporting the report

1. Scroll down to **Export & email to yourself**.
2. Choose one of:
   - **Export as PDF** — formatted report, ready to print or attach to an email.
   - **Export as CSV** — single file with all three sections, opens in any spreadsheet app.
   - **Export as Excel** — `.xls` file with separate Summary, VAT, and CIS worksheets; opens in Excel, Numbers, and Google Sheets.
3. The system share sheet appears — tap **Mail**, **AirDrop**, or any other app to send the file.
