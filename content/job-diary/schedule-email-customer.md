## How it works

1. Open the **Job Diary** tab and tap a time slot to schedule a job (or open an existing job to reschedule it).
2. Fill in the job details and select a quote with a customer email address attached.
3. Tap **Schedule & Email Customer** (or **Reschedule & Email Customer** when moving an existing booking).
4. Yoley saves the job and opens your mail app with a pre-filled message — the recipient, subject line, and body are already filled in.
5. Review the email, then tap **Send** in your mail app to deliver it to your customer.

> **Note:** Yoley opens the email but does not send it automatically. You must press **Send** inside your mail app. The email comes from your own address, so your customer sees it from you — not from a Yoley address.

## What the email contains

- Your customer's first name
- The quote reference and job title
- The scheduled start and end date/time
- Your business name

## When no email is shown

If the customer on the selected quote does not have an email address saved, the caption and pre-fill step are skipped and the button reads **Schedule Job**. Add the customer's email in the **Customers** tab before scheduling if you want to notify them.
