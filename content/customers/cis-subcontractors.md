## Adding a subcontractor

1. Tap the **menu** icon → **Subcontractors** → **+**.
2. Enter the subcontractor's **first name**, **last name**, phone, and email.

## CIS verification

Under **CIS verification**, pick how this subcontractor is set up:

| Set up as | Details required by HMRC |
|---|---|
| **Sole trader** | National Insurance number + address |
| **Limited company** | Company name + company registration number |
| **Partnership** | Partnership name + partner details |

### Sole trader
1. Enter the **National Insurance number**.
2. Enter the subcontractor's **address** (lines 1 & 2, town, postcode).

### Limited company
1. Enter the **company name** and **company registration number**, or tap **Find company on Companies House** to search.

### Partnership
1. Enter the **partnership name** and **partner details**.

## CIS details

Under **CIS Details**:
- Enter the **UTR number** (or **Partnership UTR** for a partnership).
- Set the **CIS Rate** — the outcome HMRC gave you when you verified:
  - *Gross payment status (0%)*
  - *Net payment status (20%)*
  - *Unmatched / unregistered (30%)*
- Optionally enter the **HMRC verification number** you received.
- Tap **Mark verified today** (enabled once you've entered a verification number) to record the date.

## Verifying with HMRC

HMRC doesn't provide an in-app verification API — you verify by phone or online, then record the result here.

- **Phone:** tap **Call 0300 200 3210** (the CIS helpline).
- **Online:** tap **Verify online (HMRC CIS service)** to open the HMRC Government Gateway.

Have the subcontractor's name, UTR, and National Insurance number (or company registration number) to hand before you call or log in.

> **Note:** You must verify every new subcontractor before making a payment under CIS. Keep the verification number HMRC gives you — it's your record of the verification.
