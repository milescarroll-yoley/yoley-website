Privacy Policy — Android App

Effective date: 13 September 2026  ·  Version 1.2  ·  Applies to the Yoley Android app only

Plain English summary: Your business records live on your device, not on Yoley's servers. We don't sell your data, there's no advertising and no tracking. A few features do send small amounts of information to outside services to work — checking an email address, looking up a postcode or a company, getting a weather forecast, and setting up card payments with Monek. Section 4 lists every one of them and exactly what is sent. If you import a customer from your phone's contacts, that stays on your device too. Android's own backup also copies the app's data to your Google Drive unless you turn that off.

2. What data the app handles and why

2.1 Business records you create

The app stores records you create directly on your device, including:

  • Customer contact details (name, address, email, phone)
  • Quotes, invoices, expenses, payment schedules and job diary entries
  • Receipt photos and the text extracted from them (see 2.2)

Why: To provide the core functionality of the app — you cannot use Yoley without creating these records.

2.2 Receipt scanning

When you scan a receipt, the app uses Google's ML Kit text recognition to read it. This processing happens entirely on your device — the receipt image and its text are never uploaded anywhere by Yoley.

2.3 Payment data

Yoley never sees or stores your customer's card details. When you take a payment, the app asks Monek to create a secure payment link and your customer enters their card details on Monek's own hosted checkout page — not inside Yoley. The app only records that a payment was requested and, if you mark it so, that it was received.

If you apply for a Monek merchant account from inside the app, the details you enter on that form — your name, date of birth, address, company details and bank account — are sent to Monek Merchant Services Limited, together with information from your device session, to process your application. This only happens if you choose to apply. As an FCA-authorised payment institution, Monek will check your details with a credit reference agency and verify the identity of applicants for anti-money-laundering and fraud-prevention purposes.

2.4 Sensitive fields we encrypt

Subcontractor UTR and National Insurance numbers are encrypted on your device using a key held in the Android Keystore. That key cannot leave your device, which means these fields cannot be read from a backup restored onto a different phone — you will be asked to re-enter them.

2.5 Importing a contact

When adding a customer you can choose Import from contacts. Android shows you its own contact picker and you pick one person; the app then copies that person's name, phone number, email address and postal address into a new customer record on your device. The app asks for contacts permission at that point, only reads the one contact you pick, and never uploads your address book. If you would rather not grant the permission, you can type customer details in by hand instead.

2.6 Location

Two features use your location. The job diary can show a job on a map, which uses your device's approximate location on your device only. The weather forecast needs a place to forecast for, so an approximate location is sent to our weather provider (see section 4) — if you have allowed location access that is your device's approximate position, and if you have not, it is the postcode of the business address you entered in Settings. Nothing more precise than that is sent, your location is never stored in your records, and it is never sent to Yoley. You can refuse the location permission and everything still works; the forecast simply uses your business postcode instead.

3. Where your data is stored

All business records you create are stored on your device, in a local database (Room/SQLite). There is no Yoley server, no Yoley account, and no synchronisation between devices. Yoley staff cannot see, access or recover your records.

Android backup: the app takes part in Android's standard backup, which copies the app's database and settings to your own Google Drive. This is a Google service governed by Google's privacy policy; Yoley has no access to it. It exists so you can restore your data on a new phone. You can turn it off in your device settings under System › Backup. You can also export a backup file yourself from Settings inside the app.

5. Advertising and tracking

The app contains no advertising SDKs, no third-party analytics, and no cross-app or cross-site tracking.

6. Your rights under UK GDPR

As a UK resident you have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict, or port the data held about you, and to object to processing. Because all Android app data is stored locally on your device, you can exercise most of these rights directly within the app (edit or delete records) or by deleting the app. For any other privacy questions, contact privacy@yoley.com. We will respond within 30 days.

If you are unhappy with how we handle your data, you have the right to lodge a complaint with the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk or 0303 123 1113.

7. Children's data

Yoley is a business tool intended for users aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 18.
