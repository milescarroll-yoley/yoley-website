📋 And when you do have to report, we do the paperwork

Holding nothing doesn’t mean leaving you to it. Yoley puts your figures into HMRC’s own shape: every expense is filed against its SA103 box as you scan the receipt, and you can export a boxed summary for any date range as a PDF or spreadsheet — the thing your accountant actually wants. CIS deductions are calculated and recorded per subcontractor, with UTR and rate, ready for your monthly return.

To be clear about the boundary: Yoley prepares the numbers, it doesn’t file them. CIS returns and self-assessment still go to HMRC through their portal or your accountant.

Card payments are a separate matter, and a separate company. If you choose to take cards, those transactions are processed by Monek Merchant Services Limited, an FCA-authorised payment institution which — like every acquirer, Visa and Mastercard included — keeps and must produce its own records. That is how card processing works everywhere. It says nothing about what Yoley holds, which is nothing. Full detail in the Privacy Policy.
